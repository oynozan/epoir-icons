# Epoir icon animation plan

Every icon animates on hover and replays on each interaction. The motion
returns cleanly to rest so it loops. Reveal (draw-on) is used only for marks
that are literally drawn: check, x, minus, plus, and every `-off` slash.
Arrows nudge. Everything else moves in a way that reflects the icon meaning.

## Rules baked in

- No fade (opacity) and no shrink (scale below rest) as motion. Use translation,
  real 2D and 3D rotation (rotateY with perspective), and honest path drawing.
- Signature line-draw (`drawFlow`/`drawRev`) is the technique for anything that
  flows, emanates, builds, or reveals (wind, smoke, signal, blinds, walls, a
  gleam, a bloom) as well as drawn marks (check, x, minus, plus) and every
  `-off` slash. Emanate draws flow OUT of the device toward the free tip.
- No lazy full-icon rotation where it carries no meaning (sun-moon animates
  dawn, suns shine, transgender radiates). Rotation stays only where the object
  truly turns (life-buoy, contrast disc, eclipse crescent, spinning parts).
- Every `-off` variant draws its slash from one corner to the other.
- Pivots match the anatomy: wings at the shoulder, tails at the base, heads at
  the neck, wheels and rays at their own center.
